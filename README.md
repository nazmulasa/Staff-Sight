# 👁️ Staff Sight

### Smart Employee Monitoring Software

**Smart Oversight for Modern Teams.**

**Staff Sight** is an employee monitoring and workforce visibility solution developed by [Code Astro](https://www.codeastro.dev). It helps businesses understand work activity, support productivity, and manage distributed and office-based teams.

Our goal is to provide organizations with useful workforce insights while emphasizing responsible monitoring, transparency, and data security.

## Key Features

  🖥️ **Computer Activity Monitoring** — Monitor supported employee computer activities.
  📊 **Productivity Insights** — Review activity data to better understand work patterns.
  ⏱️ **Work Time Tracking** — Track working hours and activity sessions where supported.
  👥 **Workforce Visibility** — Support the management of remote and office-based teams.
  📈 **Activity Reports** — Review available monitoring data through reports and dashboards.
  🏢 **Team Management** — Organize employee and team information.
  🔐 **Security & Access Control** — Protect monitoring data through appropriate permissions and safeguards.

*Features depend on the implemented version, platform, and configuration.*

## Who Is Staff Sight For?

- IT and software development companies
- Businesses with remote employees
- BPO and customer support teams
- Small and medium-sized enterprises
- Organizations managing distributed workforces

## Why Staff Sight?

- Improve visibility into work activities.
- Identify workflow bottlenecks.
- Support informed productivity decisions.
- Simplify workforce oversight.
- Help teams work more effectively.

## Privacy & Security

Staff Sight is designed with a security-conscious approach. Employee monitoring should be transparent, proportionate, and conducted in accordance with applicable privacy laws and organizational policies.
